# Virginia MUTCD

Apply the Virginia MUTCD to a site, device or drawing. Identify the governing authority and choose the workflow for the task. Use the source overview for edition and scope questions, and the locator to find the controlling text and graphics.

[Source overview](references/overview.md) · [Source locator](resources/source-locator.md) · [Glossary](references/glossary.md) · [Decision summary](references/cheatsheet.md)

## Review sequence

1. Start with the jurisdiction, public travel status, project date and controlling edition. Cite the incorporated PDF and relevant agency source for a current determination.
2. Classify each provision as Standard, Guidance, Option or Support. Read the actual predicates, exceptions and table or figure notes with the governing text.
3. For every finding, record the facility or device, controlling citation, evidence, result, and specific unresolved input or follow-up.
4. Check official PDF graphics before using dimensions, graphical thresholds, color, or struck text. Treat errata as evidence of a discrepancy and check its publication status.
5. Use the responsible authority's engineering study, approvals, and field inputs where the selected workflow calls for them.

Read the primary workflow first. For a related task, use the [capability index](references/capability-index.md) to follow a cross-reference. For requests limited to a named provision or definition, use the official source and glossary directly.

## Findings

Use a short decision or review record. Include the result, provision and statement class, supporting inputs, and open issues. For calculations, show the branch, input values, units, substitution, and result. For field or plan reviews, use one row per device, location or movement.

- Give a conditional finding when jurisdiction, adoption, edition, inputs, or official figure evidence remain unresolved.
- Give installation and operation findings at the level supported by the engineering, authority and field records.
