# Virginia MUTCD Distillation and Use

This skill uses the Virginia Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices for Streets and Highways, Version 11.0, January 2026, issued by the Virginia Department of Transportation. It supports source review, screening, calculations, and preparation of device findings on Virginia facilities. Read the governing paragraph, exceptions and official visuals for current project decisions.

## Where to look

- Notice and Introduction, PDF pp. 2–4. Governing copies, jurisdiction, Virginia editorial conventions, and edition transition.
- Part 1, PDF pp. 37–76. Applicability, classes of statements, engineering responsibility and official request processes.
- Part 2, PDF pp. 77–616. Sign application, design, installation and maintenance. Appendix A1, pp. 1003–1004, includes legislative context and an applicable lettering provision.
- Parts 3 and 4, PDF pp. 617–846. Markings, signal warrants, equipment, timing, accessibility and related controls.
- Parts 5 and 6, PDF pp. 847–854. Uniform, serviceable devices relevant to automation and the Virginia Work Area Protection Manual handoff.
- Parts 7–9, PDF pp. 855–1000. School areas, grade crossings, and bicycle facilities. These parts cross-reference ordinary device chapters.
- Appendix A2, PDF p. 1005. Approximate conversions. Preserve original units and check calculations against the stated conversion factor. Some extracted rows have apparent discrepancies, including the 130 ft entry and duplicate 65 mph entries.

## Source checks

The Virginia Administrative Code distinguishes National MUTCD coverage and the Virginia standard for facilities under VDOT jurisdiction. The Introduction explains local adoption and allowances. Verify the facility, applicable project date, maintained jurisdiction, and any relevant adoption documentation. [General provisions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title24/agency30/chapter315/section10/) and [incorporated Virginia manuals](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title24/agency30/chapter315/section9999/).

The supplied version's Introduction paragraph 13 names January 1, 2026. VDOT's Road Design Manual page identifies January 18, 2026 as the effective date of Version 11.0. For work near the transition, confirm the adopted document and the authority's applicable project record. [VDOT Road Design Manual](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/doing-business/technical-guidance-and-support/technical-guidance-documents/road-design-manual/).

VDOT publishes the edition, Virginia and federal known-error links, and sign design references. Its November 10, 2026 Virginia list describes informational corrections planned for a future revision. Use its stated status and verify the current authority's published disposition before relying on an affected entry. [VDOT Virginia MUTCD portal](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/doing-business/technical-guidance-and-support/technical-guidance-documents/virginia-mutcd/).

For PDF pages, sections, tables, and figures, use the [source locator](../resources/source-locator.md). The locator reflects the supplied edition's pagination. Verify individual source graphics and any discrepancy by the governing ID and printed page. The glossary provides terms used in the cards.
