# Review device uniformity and visibility for driving automation

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.automation`

## R — Source

§5A.04, PDF pp. 848–850; §§5B.01–5B.06, PDF pp. 850–851; cross-references §§1D.10, 2A.19, 3A.05, 4A.10.

## I — Interpretation

Part 5 describes risks from inconsistent or degraded equipment and recommends uniform application and maintenance for human and machine perception. The governing device criteria stay in the base chapters and specialized parts.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. A corridor inventory records low daytime marking contrast and a damaged warning sign. Log the two conditions under §§5B.01–02, pair each with its device maintenance section, and assign inspection/remediation priority using observed severity.

## A2 — When to use

Review a Virginia roadway device inventory for uniformity, condition and legibility relevant to automated and human driving.

Use the underlying sign, marking, signal, rail, bicycle or work-zone card for a specific device decision.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Comparable roadway locations, device inventory, placement and condition observations, light and contrast conditions, known outages, and the user's maintenance goals.

1. Compare application and placement at comparable sites. Identify degraded, damaged, extraneous or inconsistent equipment. Record observed conditions and evidence.
2. Screen the applicable Part 5B category. For signs consider visibility, legibility and presentation. For markings consider contrast, conspicuity and continuity. For signals consider consistent and visible displays. For temporary areas, rail and bicycle situations record the actual cue and operating context.
3. Attach each finding to a base device provision or identify it as Part 5 Guidance or Support. Assign the responsible maintenance workstream and specific observation or repair for follow-up.
4. Return a prioritized inventory with evidence, provision, maintenance action and verification required.

**Deliverable** — Device-condition matrix for a maintenance or corridor review.

## B — Boundaries

Part 5 supports device maintenance and uniform application. It supplies no performance certification for an automated driving system or for a vehicle in a specific operating domain.
