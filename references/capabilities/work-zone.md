# Identify the governing temporary traffic control manual

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.work-zone`

## R — Source

Introduction paragraph 02, PDF p. 3; §1A.04, PDF p. 38; Part 6, PDF p. 853; [official incorporation register](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title24/agency30/chapter315/section9999/).

## I — Interpretation

Virginia incorporates the Work Area Protection Manual (VWAPM) as its physically separate Part 6. Determine agency and edition before evaluating layouts or temporary traffic control devices.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. A VDOT-maintained utility work site needs a temporary lane closure. Record the VWAPM authority and project/permit dates, then request the applicable plan or section and agency transition evidence for the layout review.

## A2 — When to use

Identify the applicable temporary traffic control or work-zone manual for a Virginia lane closure, project, permit or event.

Use lane reduction for an eligible permanent through-lane taper.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Location, maintaining authority, activity type, operation date, contract advertisement or land-use permit date, and relevant local adoption or transition documentation.

1. Establish jurisdiction and controlling source with the authority card. For VDOT use the current incorporated VWAPM and review the applicable official transition guidance and project records. For other authorities check the national and local requirements that govern the facility.
2. Record the applicable reference, version, project date and basis. Obtain the relevant VWAPM section and figure before assessing layout, signs, taper lengths, or devices.
3. If project dates or governing documents are missing, give the applicable or potential references and identify the controlling evidence to obtain. If the user only requests the governing manual, deliver the source handoff once authority is resolved.

**Deliverable** — Authority and edition handoff with the needed source or specific open issue.

## B — Boundaries

The supplied Virginia MUTCD contains a Part 6 referral. Temporary layout methods and design values require the applicable VWAPM or other controlling local source.
