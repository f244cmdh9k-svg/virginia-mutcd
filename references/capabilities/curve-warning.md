# Screen horizontal-curve warnings

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.curve-warning`

## R — Source

§2C.06 and Table 2C-4, PDF pp. 211–212; §2C.59 and Table 2C-6, PDF pp. 259–260.

## I — Interpretation

Chart A classifies need by road class, AADT, and pavement markings (center line or edge lines or both) where applicable. Chart B uses the difference between the established approach speed under the section and the advisory speed established by study. Separate each device's statement status.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. A paved, center-lined collector carrying 4,200 AADT has a documented approach speed of 45 mph and advisory speed of 30 mph. Chart A makes devices required for this site. Chart B uses a 15 mph differential. Record required delineators, an advance horizontal alignment warning sign and the W13-1P advisory speed plaque, subject to the stated exceptions.

## A2 — When to use

Select or audit Virginia horizontal-alignment curve-warning devices, classify the Table 2C-4 chart branch, or evaluate advisory-speed evidence.

Use sign review for sign sizing and placement.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Road class, paving, center line, AADT, approach-speed inputs, documented advisory speed, edge lines, installation context, and applicable curve geometry.

1. Use §2C.06 and Chart A to classify need. Use Chart B when Chart A indicates devices. Check the separate treatment for freeways, expressways, ramps, roundabouts, and the specific exceptions. Record the chart basis.
2. Obtain the §2C.59 engineering-study advisory speed, including the supported test method when applicable, and select the approach speed under §2C.06. Subtract advisory from approach speed in mph. Match the exact Chart B thresholds and retain each required, recommended or optional row as printed.
3. Confirm the particular W1 device, auxiliary plaque, delineators/chevrons and locations using their sections, the figure, and the ¶¶03, 05 and 06 exceptions. Check the W13-1P requirement under §2C.59 if the warning sign is used under §2C.06. Use the official PDF for graphical markings and Table 2C-6 when a ball-bank reading is involved.
4. Return chart and threshold, inputs and calculation, the device schedule with each item's classification, and any engineering or field follow-up.

**Deliverable** — Curve-warning screening worksheet and sign schedule handoff.

## B — Boundaries

An undocumented advisory speed blocks a numeric result. Thresholds and exceptions apply to the defined roadway and curve context. Use separate device instructions for siting and spacing.
