# Screen a pedestrian crossing and supplemental devices

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.pedestrian-crossing`

## R — Source

§§3C.01–3C.05, PDF pp. 672–676; §§4J.01–4J.03, PDF pp. 814–817; §§4L.01–4L.03, PDF pp. 823–826; applicable Virginia IIM-TE-384 as identified in §4L.01 ¶01a.

## I — Interpretation

An uncontrolled marked crossing calls for site evaluation. The §3C.02 ¶06 conditions support a decision about supplemental measures. RRFBs and pedestrian hybrid beacons have distinct application criteria. Crosswalk marking design follows the applicable Chapter 3C and specialized-chapter provisions.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. An uncontrolled four-lane crossing without a refuge island at ADT 13,000 enters the §3C.02 ¶06 Guidance for measures beyond crosswalk markings alone. This threshold identifies a treatment need for study, with the device choice still open.

## A2 — When to use

Review an uncontrolled pedestrian crossing, marked crosswalk treatment, or preliminary RRFB or pedestrian hybrid beacon eligibility.

Use school area for a school route and signal warrants for a conventional traffic signal investigation.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Approach control, speed limit, lanes crossed and traffic volume, crossing length, refuge, sight distance and parking, user volumes and characteristics, gaps and crash data, nearby control and proposed device.

1. Identify the crossing and approaches individually. For an uncontrolled location, review the engineering-study considerations in §3C.02 and any applicable Virginia pedestrian-crossing policy. Apply the §3C.02 ¶05 Standard for markings at legally established non-intersection crosswalks. Record measured limits and conditions.
2. Apply §3C.02 ¶06. Screen four or more lanes without a raised median or pedestrian refuge island and ADT at least 12,000, four or more with a raised median or pedestrian refuge island and ADT at least 15,000, posted speeds at least 40 mph, predominant multiple-threat crashes on a multilane approach, and inadequate visibility after parking measures. Record each true, false or unknown branch and the Guidance consequence.
3. If considering an RRFB, check the marked crossing, permitted warning sign assembly and approach controls in §4L.01. Apply the specific roundabout and free-flow-turn exceptions only when the stated geometry is present. For a hybrid beacon, evaluate §4J.01's applicable criteria and charts with the official graphic.
4. Confirm design and operation in the appropriate chapter once eligibility and treatment selection are documented. Record mitigation and alternatives with the resulting evidence.

**Deliverable** — Crossing screen, cited design/operations handoff and data gaps. State which treatment findings are conditional.

## B — Boundaries

A supportive threshold does not choose a device automatically. At an ordinary STOP-, YIELD-, signal- or PHB-controlled approach, the RRFB restriction applies unless the exact §4L.01 ¶04 exception is demonstrated.
