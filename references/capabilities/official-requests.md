# Choose an official MUTCD request pathway

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.official-requests`

## R — Source

§§1B.04–1B.08, PDF pp. 43–49; §1D.06, PDF pp. 71–72.

## I — Interpretation

Interpretation clarifies existing text, experimentation evaluates field use, a change seeks revision of the manual, and interim approval follows the specific terms and applicability of an existing or proposed approval. Each path has its own submission and authorization conditions.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. An agency proposes to field test a novel device application without any identified approval. Select the experimentation path, record the entity with jurisdiction, propose evaluation and restoration procedures, and list the required prior permissions.

## A2 — When to use

Route an official interpretation, experimentation, rule-change, or interim-approval request about a traffic control device in Virginia.

Use authority for a provision that can be resolved directly from controlling text.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Site, operating or maintaining authority, exact device/application, cited provision, requested outcome, candidate FHWA interpretation or interim approval and Virginia status.

1. Select the matching section from §§1B.04–1B.07. For an already authorized device, pull the exact approval and its eligibility, use and reporting conditions. Check Virginia adoption or approval and the submitting authority before proposing implementation.
2. For interpretation, capture the provision, facts and ambiguity in a focused question. For a change, identify exact target provisions, proposed changes and supporting research or test data. For interim approval, distinguish the request to issue one from a request to use a published approval and collect the applicable commitments.
3. For an experiment, check §1B.05 and assemble the problem statement, device details, proposition, locations, duration, evaluation and reporting plan, removal or restoration commitments, and the required device-rights certifications. Verify the Virginia submittal or approval step and FHWA instructions in §1B.08. Follow §1B.03 ¶¶10a–10b for contact with FHWA Headquarters and the Virginia Division office.
4. Produce a pathway memo with the necessary signatures or agency approvals, evidence still missing and authorization status. Keep a proposal at draft status until the controlling authorization is documented.

**Deliverable** — Request type, recipient and authority, section-specific evidence checklist and unresolved conditions.

## B — Boundaries

Submission details and approval status are time-sensitive. Implementation depends on the approval terms, jurisdiction, and responsible agency. Use of this card produces a draft for review by the submitting authority.
