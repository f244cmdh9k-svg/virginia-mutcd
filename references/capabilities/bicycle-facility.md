# Review bicycle traffic control at conflict points

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.bicycle-facility`

## R — Source

Chapter 9A, PDF pp. 933–937; Chapters 9B–9F, PDF pp. 938–1000; §9E.02, PDF pp. 974–981; §§9E.11–9E.12 and applicable 9B/9D signing; Chapter 4H where authorized.

## I — Interpretation

Traffic control depends on facility class and maneuver. At turn-only lane conflicts §9E.02 ¶01 is the baseline. Its ¶02 exception requires bicycle signal indications. Virginia's ¶01a flags legal limits as of publication, so current authority matters for an exception or other bicycle-signal proposal.

## A1 — Examples

Source illustrations. Figure 9B-5 shows the regulatory series for two-stage turns and Figure 9D-7 shows guide signage. Their applicability requires their governing provisions.

Synthetic exercise. A plan keeps the through bicycle lane immediately to the right of a mandatory right-turn-only lane, with ordinary vehicular signals only. Record a conflict with §9E.02 ¶01. Request lane-layout revision or evidence of an authorized signal exception.

## A2 — When to use

Audit bicycle signs, markings, turn-lane conflicts, shared-use path crossings, turn boxes or detector markings in a Virginia plan.

Use vehicle signal design and pedestrian crossing as needed for linked intersection treatments.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Facility class, travel direction and maneuver, scaled approach and receiving geometry, motor-vehicle turn lanes, crossing and signal control, signing and marking schedule, speeds and governing bicycle-signal authority.

1. Classify bicycle lane, buffer-separated/separated or counter-flow lane, shared lane or path. Identify all conflict points and the movement at each. Locate the corresponding application sections in 9A, 9B–9F and the governing base chapters.
2. At intersection approaches compare the through bicycle lane to mandatory turn-only lanes under §9E.02. Evaluate the exception only with an actual bicycle indication and verified current jurisdictional authorization. Review lane-drop and shared-lane options under their exact conditions.
3. Verify the marking or sign device provision and the official figure geometry. Treat a two-stage turn box (§9E.11) and a bicycle box (§9E.12) as different devices. Check the relevant regulatory/guide signing, signal or detection and crossing implications. Consult current errata for affected figures, including Figure 9E-2(VA).
4. Return conflict-point findings with drawing and citation, evidence for exceptions, and the specific drawing, legal or field change needed.

**Deliverable** — A bicycle traffic-control review matrix keyed to facility segment and maneuver.

## B — Boundaries

The source contains publication-dated legal notes and example figures. Verify current authority and measured site geometry before applying the bicycle-signal exception or relying on figure callouts.
