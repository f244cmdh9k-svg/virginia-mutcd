# Calculate a permanent lane-reduction taper

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.lane-reduction`

## R — Source

§3B.12, including ¶¶08–12, and Figure 3B-14(VA), PDF pp. 648–649.

## I — Interpretation

The speed limit selects the formula branch. S in the chosen formula is the greater of speed limit and 85th-percentile speed. The formula in the Guidance yields taper length L in feet, with offset W in feet and S in mph. Virginia ¶08a supplies a conditional speed estimate when measured 85th-percentile data are unavailable. Review the separate recommended urban/rural lower bounds and the low-operating-speed exception.

## A1 — Examples

Source illustration. Figure 3B-14(VA) locates the taper and associated warning treatments. Confirm its official geometry and numbered notes visually.

Synthetic calculation. Posted 40 mph, 85th-percentile 45 mph, W = 12 ft. The under-45 mph formula gives 12 × 45² / 60 = 405 ft.

## A2 — When to use

Calculate or audit a length and marking arrangement for a permanent through-lane reduction outside an intersection or interchange.

Route temporary work-zone tapers to work zone and unrelated marking details to marking review.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Permanent/temporary status, context, effective speed limit, measured 85th-percentile speed, lateral offset and units, urban/rural context and plan geometry.

1. Confirm that a through lane is being reduced under §3B.12 and check that the location is outside an intersection or interchange. Record the governing section and verify the official Figure 3B-14(VA).
2. Set S to the greater of the effective speed limit and 85th-percentile speed. Where the 85th-percentile speed is unavailable and is expected to exceed the speed limit, the Virginia ¶08a Option permits speed limit plus 5 mph. Record evidence for that expectation and label the resulting estimate. If a new roadway has no established speed limit, review the design-speed Option in ¶12 and verify how the authority applies the formula. Evaluate L = W × S where the speed limit is at least 45 mph, or L = W × S² / 60 where the speed limit is below 45 mph. Show substitution and unrounded result with units.
3. Check the Guidance that L should be at least 100 ft urban or 200 ft rural and its low-operating-speed exception. Record the Guidance status of the formula and recommended floors. Review Virginia's lane-reduction arrow Standard at a speed limit of 45 mph or greater, the advance-warning application, and associated markings.
4. Report the branch, measured inputs, calculation, applicable lower bounds, and cited sign and marking follow-up. If a governing input is missing, give a conditional calculation with its assumptions and any unresolved evidence. A lower-bound calculation using the speed limit alone does not establish the design length.

**Deliverable** — Traceable taper calculation and associated device review.

## B — Boundaries

Confirm taper applicability and geometry before giving a design finding. Installed speed and site data can change the result. The unit convention is integral to both formulas.
