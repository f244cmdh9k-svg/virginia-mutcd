# Screen conventional traffic signal warrants

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.signal-warrants`

## R — Source

§§4B.03, 4C.01–4C.10, PDF pp. 733–751; §4C.01 ¶¶03, 03a, 05a and 20a. Check the current IIM-TOD-397 and applicable IIM-TE-387.

## I — Interpretation

Virginia requires at least one warrant and separate justification for a new signal under §4C.01. A count threshold or chart result is one part of the installation decision. Alternatives, safety and operation require a distinct engineering record.

## A1 — Examples

Source worked example. In §4C.10 ¶04B, PDF p. 750, a 95 ft actual distance uses the D = 90 ft curve under the section's curve-selection rule. That example establishes the curve choice. Compare volumes separately.

Synthetic exercise. An established school crossing has 24 schoolchildren in the highest crossing hour, 12 adequate gaps in a 20-minute crossing period and the nearest major-street signal 600 ft away. The basic §4C.06 numerical screen is met. Confirm the engineering study, alternatives, and pedestrian signal heads if a signal is justified.

## A2 — When to use

Screen conventional Virginia traffic signal warrants or completeness of a proposed intersection control study.

Use signal design for the plan and timing of a selected signal, and grade crossing for the diagnostic and preemption workstream.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Scope, study period, classified turning movements and hours, approach lane use and geometry, speeds and population where relevant, pedestrian and school counts and gaps, crash history, coordination and network context, and nearest rail/other controls.

1. Confirm that §4C.01 applies. List warrants 1–9 and mark each as applicable, inapplicable with reason, or unresolved. Identify alternate control strategies and the current statewide assessment policy.
2. For each applicable warrant apply exactly the stated lane, hour, counting, comparison, and adjustment rules in §§4C.02–10. Document engineering judgment on turns and effective lanes. Select the actual table or curve using its speed, population, length or rail criteria and preserve units and threshold comparisons. Verify curves visually.
3. For Warrant 1, keep hourly and average-daily criteria separate. Use the same eight hours for the major and minor counts within each evaluated condition. Apply the ADT projection path in Virginia §4C.02 ¶¶12a–12g only if its prerequisites hold. For Warrant 5 check the established crossing, adequacy of gaps during the crossing period, the minimum of 20 schoolchildren during the highest crossing hour, and the nearby-signal Guidance. Consider remedial measures and pedestrian accommodations under §4C.06.
4. Report met, unmet, or unknown for each warrant and show the independent engineering justification and alternative-control record. Verify the Virginia Standard in ¶03a against surrounding Support. Without the required warrant and substantiated justification, withhold an installation finding.

**Deliverable** — Cited warrant worksheet, data gaps, and separate justification and alternatives findings.

## B — Boundaries

The warrant worksheet is screening evidence. It depends on accurate counts, site facts and official tables or figures. Temporary-zone and special-purpose signal controls use their applicable device chapters and authorities.
