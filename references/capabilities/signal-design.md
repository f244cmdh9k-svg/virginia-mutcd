# Audit signal design, operation, and accessibility

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.signal-design`

## R — Source

Part 4, PDF pp. 727–846. Chapter 4D covers placement, 4E indications and faces, 4F steady operation, 4G flashing operation, 4H bicycle signal faces, 4I pedestrian indications and intervals, 4K accessible pedestrian signals, and later chapters special controls. See §§4B.04–4B.05 for part-time operation and signal removal.

## I — Interpretation

Signal design and operation depend on the control type, road users, movements, geometry, phase relationships, and mode. Chapter 4D geometry checks and Chapter 4F display/operations checks address related but different design conditions. For a special purpose device address its own application, design and operation provisions.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. A drawing shows motor vehicle signal faces and a marked pedestrian crossing, but omits the pedestrian phase and accessible pushbutton detail. Identify Chapters 4I and 4K and request the phase/timing and field geometry data before clearing the crossing provisions.

## A2 — When to use

Audit Virginia signal plans, indications, phasing, timing, accessibility, special signal controls, or existing signal removal.

Use signal warrants for whether a conventional signal is justified, and grade crossing for preemption and track clearance.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Control type, scaled plan and signal face schedule, lane and crossing configuration, approach speeds, phasing and conflict plan, timing, detection and pedestrian/accessible details, preemption requirements and study records.

1. Classify the device and operation. Use Chapters 4D–4G for ordinary vehicle faces, 4H–4K for bicycle/pedestrian needs as applicable, and the appropriate special-purpose chapter for beacons, emergency access, one-lane facilities, ramps, reversible facilities or other controls.
2. For each approach record face count, alignment, visibility, offset, height and aims. Compare to the applicable §4D criteria and official figures. Review color, shape, sequence and housing against 4E and check every movement's permitted display in steady and flashing modes under 4F and 4G as applicable.
3. Check pedestrian and accessible location, detection, indication and timing using actual inputs and Chapters 4I and 4K. Trace preemption priorities and transitions against the governing sections and confirm track interfaces with the Diagnostic Team when relevant.
4. For removal or part-time operation, use the relevant 4B procedure and existing operations, volumes, safety and study evidence. Document temporary and final treatments wherever the source calls for them.
5. Return keyed plan findings and required corrections with clauses, input gaps, and verification status.

**Deliverable** — Approach and movement-based plan review plus unresolved design and operational items.

## B — Boundaries

Unknown timing, dimensions or hardware leave the affected findings open. Use the governing device-specific clauses and applicable VDOT policy or specifications for numerical design and agency review.
