# Review pavement markings and maintenance

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.marking-review`

## R — Source

§§3A.01–3A.05, PDF pp. 617–620; §§3B.01–3B.03, PDF pp. 620–624; Chapters 3B–3K for detailed applications. Confirm a chapter's actual heading and range in the source locator.

## I — Interpretation

The lane or area function controls color and pattern. Where §3B.02 applies, distinguish Virginia's center-line installation standards from its engineering-study and engineering-judgment branches. The longitudinal retroreflectivity provision evaluates implementation and continued use of a maintenance method. Its dry measurement values, exclusions, and exceptions differ from a blanket acceptance criterion for every marking.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. A paved, undivided, two-way collector with a 20 ft traveled way and ADT 800, outside the stated residential or industrial exception, enters the center-line Standard. A white longitudinal line on a 45 mph road enters the dry retroreflectivity maintenance-method screen when no exceptions or exclusions apply. The baseline Standard target is 50 mcd/m²/lx (§3A.05 ¶01).

## A2 — When to use

Audit a Virginia pavement marking plan, decide if a yellow center line is required, or review longitudinal marking retroreflectivity.

Use lane reduction for taper calculations, pedestrian crossing for crossing treatment, and bicycle facility for Part 9 details.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Lane purpose and travel direction, surface, traveled-way width, roadway classification, speed, traffic, proposed pattern or material and maintenance method. Include measured values and conditions when available.

1. Inventory center, lane, edge, channelizing, crosswalk, word/symbol, raised marker, delineator, island, curb, and colored treatments as present. Locate §§3A.01–04 and the relevant application chapter. Record color, pattern, width, gap, placement and governing clause. Check the current known-error list when the cited entry is flagged.
2. For yellow center lines, apply §3B.02's separate branches. Read Table 3B-V1 for the road-specific cross-check. Check the paved two-way arterial/collector branch with traveled way at least 18 ft and ADT at least 500, its residential-street and industrial-complex exception, and the separate three-or-more motor travel lane branch. For lower volume or narrow roads, read the Option and Guidance directly. Evaluate required no-passing zones separately under §3B.03.
3. For longitudinal retroreflectivity, check the §3A.05 ¶05 exceptions and ¶06 exclusions. Record the 35 mph Standard threshold with target 50 and the 70 mph Guidance threshold with target 100, in mcd/m²/lx under dry conditions at the reference geometry. Record the method and how it is used. For inspection results, preserve the measurement context.
4. Return findings by segment or marking type with proposed and required values, statement class, source and action.

**Deliverable** — Marking matrix, maintenance-method review and list of missing records.

## B — Boundaries

The source's known errors include several marking and marker entries. Verify the official text and any applicable agency decision before using their values as binding design inputs. Do not substitute Part 3's permanent taper rules for temporary traffic control.
