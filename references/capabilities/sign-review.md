# Review a permanent sign schedule

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.sign-review`

## R — Source

Chapter 2A, §§2A.02–2A.22, PDF pp. 77–102; applicable Chapter 2B–2N device section and family-specific size table. For a STOP sign, consult §2B.04 and Table 2B-1(VA).

## I — Interpretation

Uniform coding, design, message and placement support consistent recognition. Sections 2A.07 and 2A.13–2A.22 generally govern dimensions, location, mounting, maintenance, and nighttime appearance. The device-specific application governs its purpose and exceptions.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. A regulatory sign schedule lists an R1-1 with no roadway class, dimensions, or mounting detail. Record the sign type and missing physical inputs, then identify the applicable size table and Chapter 2A placement checks.

## A2 — When to use

Review a permanent Virginia regulatory, warning, guide, service, or other Part 2 sign schedule for selection, size, installation, or maintenance.

Use curve warning for alignment chart decisions, school area for school treatments, and work zone for temporary signing.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Sign code and legend, purpose, roadway class and lane count, approach speed as relevant, roadway and sign geometry, drawings, and maintenance or retroreflectivity observations.

1. Identify the sign family in Part 2 and cite the exact application section and any specialized sign provisions in Parts 7–9. Consult Appendix A1 for the applicable Series E(modified)-Alternate lettering provisions where relevant. Check that the sign is appropriate for the actual restriction, hazard or destination and its application conditions. For an all-way stop proposal, evaluate §§2B.12–2B.17 and their study predicates before reviewing the signs.
2. Record the permitted code, legend, shape, color, and dimensions. For sizing, select the road-category column and check notes, special size rules, plaque size, and any Virginia edits or reported discrepancies. Consult the official sign design specification for fabrication details.
3. Check location and grouping, advance placement where relevant (§2C.04 and Table 2C-3 for warning signs), sight lines, lateral offset, mounting height, orientation, support and crashworthiness. Separate Standard dimensions from Guidance and Options.
4. Check physical condition, day/night legibility, illumination or retroreflection and the agency's method under §2A.22. Use the applicable Table 2A-5 category and exclusions to assess the method. Record whether the agency uses its method separately from the condition of an individual sign.
5. Return a row per sign with the proposed value, provision, evidence, status and action. Keep field measurement and document gaps open.

**Deliverable** — Cited sign schedule with separate findings for application, size, placement and maintenance.

## B — Boundaries

For numerical findings, verify the controlling table column, governing text, figure notes and current known errors in the official references. A sign code alone supplies neither size nor field placement.
