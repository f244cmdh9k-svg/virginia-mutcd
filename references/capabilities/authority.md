# Establish authority and interpret a provision

**Capability ID** — `cap.virginia-mutcd.authority`

## R — Source

Notice and Introduction, PDF pp. 2–4; §§1A.04, 1B.01–1B.03, 1C.01, 1D.02–1D.03, PDF pp. 38–43, 50, 68–70.

Current authority is independently checked against [24VAC30-315-10](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title24/agency30/chapter315/section10/) and the [incorporation register](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title24/agency30/chapter315/section9999/).

## I — Interpretation

A Standard binds its stated case. Guidance describes a recommended practice with room for documented engineering judgment or study. An Option authorizes a course of action under its stated conditions. Support explains context. The section's heading and operative words govern, while the Virginia color identifies the origin of a change. Governing text determines the force of its figure, table or notes.

## A1 — Examples

Synthetic exercise. A public VDOT-maintained arterial is evaluated against the incorporated Virginia edition. A separate locally maintained road requires evidence of local adoption and the applicable national and state authorities. A PDF dimension marked as Virginia-specific still needs its governing paragraph and table note.

## A2 — When to use

Determine which Virginia or national traffic-control authority applies, interpret shall/should/may, or establish device replacement and compliance dates.

Use the official-request card for interpretation requests and experimental use. Use the work-zone card for the Part 6 handoff.

## E — Execution

**Inputs** — Location, ownership and maintenance jurisdiction, public travel status, local adoption evidence, project and installation or replacement dates, device context, and proposed source citation.

1. Classify the site as an agency-jurisdiction roadway, a locally maintained roadway, a private site roadway open to public travel, or another facility. Check the regulatory scope, §§1B.01–02, Introduction paragraphs 03–07, and local adoption records. Resolve uncertain jurisdiction with the maintaining authority. Check exclusions such as parking areas and their aisles under §1C.02.
2. Open the current incorporated document and agency revision or errata information. Record edition, applicability date, and any project-specific transition or published compliance date. For existing equipment, distinguish new installation, replacement, and a specific compliance date under §1B.03 and Table 1B-1. For a proposed in-kind replacement of a noncompliant device, verify ¶¶12–13, the applicable Virginia condition, and the documented engineering judgment.
3. Read the full paragraph and surrounding headings, replacements, exceptions and notes. Check the official visual for deletions, mixed text, tables and dimensions. Treat a published known-error notice as a discrepancy requiring resolution according to its stated status.
4. Record the operative clause, class, facts that trigger it, conclusion, and supporting references. Where §1D.03 permits a limited site-specific deviation from a Standard because compliance is impossible or impracticable, verify agency authority and the fully documented engineering study. If evidence conflicts or is unavailable, produce a conditional finding naming the fact or authority needed.

**Deliverable** — Applicability and clause matrix with a status for each question, an explicit edition and project date, and citations to paragraph/table/figure as applicable.

## B — Boundaries

This is decision support. The agency establishes the governing project version and resolves ambiguities. The supplied Introduction ¶13 and VDOT's Road Design Manual page give different January 2026 dates. For transition cases, obtain the official applicability record. A website update date, document cover date, and regulatory history date describe different events.
